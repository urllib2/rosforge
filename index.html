The Roadmap

What You Will Build

A structured path from zero to a fully autonomous system.

Week 1

ROS2 Fundamentals

Nodes, topics, messages. Drive a robot and observe the system in action.

Week 2

Robot Modeling

TF, RViz, URDF. Build and package your own robot from scratch.

Week 3

Physics Simulation

Gazebo: spawn your robot in a simulator with gravity, friction and collisions.

Week 4

SLAM & Mapping

Drive a TurtleBot3 through an environment and generate a 2D map with SLAM Toolbox.

Week 5

Nav2 Stack

Autonomous navigation: tune costmaps and inflation radius, handle recovery behaviors.

Week 6

Final Project

The full autonomous stack, end to end. Map, clean, navigate. GitHub portfolio ready.
